# Undergraduate Research

## Title

Night-Time Food Culture Development and Its Implications for Urban Planning

--------------------------------------------------

## Research Overview

This research investigated the emergence and spatial distribution of night-time food activity nodes within the Colombo Municipal Council area.

The study examined how these activity nodes influence accessibility, urban vitality, economic opportunities, social interaction, and planning practices.

The objective was to understand the spatial characteristics of night-time urban activities and develop planning recommendations that support sustainable and well-managed night-time economies.

--------------------------------------------------

## Research Objectives

Identify the spatial distribution of night-time food activity nodes.

Understand the factors influencing their development.

Evaluate accessibility and urban connectivity.

Assess social, economic, and planning implications.

Develop planning recommendations for sustainable management.

# Frequently Asked Questions

Question

What is your main research interest?

Answer

My primary research interest lies in integrating urban planning with geospatial technologies and spatial analytics to better understand urban systems and support evidence-based planning. I am particularly interested in urban informatics, GIS, transportation planning, urban mobility, and sustainable development.

--------------------------------------------------

Question

Tell me about your undergraduate research.

Answer

My undergraduate research examined the emergence of night-time food culture developments within the Colombo Municipal Council area and their implications for urban planning. Using GIS, field surveys, stakeholder interviews, and advanced spatial modelling techniques such as Logistic Regression, GWR, and SCCM, I investigated how these urban activity nodes influence accessibility, economic vitality, and planning decisions. The research resulted in evidence-based recommendations for managing and planning sustainable night-time urban environments.

--------------------------------------------------
