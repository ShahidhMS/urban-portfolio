## Overview

This project involved developing a heritage-led urban regeneration proposal for the Old Town Hall and Market Precinct in Pettah, Colombo. The objective was to revitalize one of Colombo's most historically significant commercial districts by integrating heritage conservation, public space enhancement, multimodal mobility, and economic revitalization.

--------------------------------------------------

## Problem Statement

The study area experiences numerous urban challenges, including deteriorating public spaces, pedestrian congestion, conflicts between formal and informal activities, declining heritage character, inefficient circulation, and limited public realm quality.

The project sought to create a comprehensive regeneration strategy that balances heritage conservation with contemporary urban development needs.
