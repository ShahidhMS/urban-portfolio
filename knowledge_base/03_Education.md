## About the Degree

The BSc (Hons) in Urban Informatics and Planning combines traditional urban planning with modern geospatial technologies, data analytics, urban informatics, and digital planning approaches.

The programme provides interdisciplinary knowledge in planning, geography, environmental management, spatial technologies, urban design, transportation, policy analysis, and research methods.

It emphasizes evidence-based planning supported by Geographic Information Systems (GIS), Remote Sensing, spatial modelling, programming, and quantitative analysis.

Answer:
