## Who am I?

Hi, I'm Shahidh Saliheen, an Urban Planning graduate from the University of Moratuwa with a strong passion for creating sustainable, resilient, and people-centered cities through data-driven planning and geospatial technologies.

My academic and professional journey has focused on integrating urban planning principles with Geographic Information Systems (GIS), Remote Sensing, Spatial Analytics, urban research, and emerging technologies to better understand cities and support evidence-based decision-making.

I enjoy solving complex urban challenges by combining planning knowledge with spatial data, analytical thinking, and modern geospatial tools.

--------------------------------------------------

## Professional Profile

I specialize in applying GIS, spatial analysis, remote sensing, and planning methodologies to support urban development, infrastructure planning, heritage conservation, transportation planning, and environmental management.

My experience includes academic research, consultancy projects, municipal planning, drone surveying, land development, urban design, and geospatial data analysis.

I have worked collaboratively with planners, GIS specialists, surveyors, engineers, architects, and multidisciplinary project teams to deliver planning and development solutions.
